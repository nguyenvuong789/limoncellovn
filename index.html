Our Story

Our Story.

Our History

Italian tradition,
settled in Đà Nẵng

Since 2014, Limoncello has welcomed guests into a dining room built around honest Italian cooking and easy conversation. The idea has always been simple: serve food with care, make people feel looked after, and let the table do the rest.

Our pizzas are prepared by hand and baked with care. Our pasta is made fresh, our sauces are given time, and our kitchen keeps close to the flavours that made Italian food loved around the world.

Imported cheeses, cured meats, olive oils, and Italian staples sit alongside the freshness of Vietnam's coast and markets. The result feels unmistakably Italian, but completely at home in Đà Nẵng.

Why Limoncello

What we stand for

Real Italian Cooking
We keep the food grounded in Italian technique: simple ingredients, careful timing, and no unnecessary fuss.
Warm Hospitality
Service should feel attentive without feeling formal: genuine, relaxed, and ready with a good recommendation.
